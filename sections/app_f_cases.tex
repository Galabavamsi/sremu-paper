\subsection{E-B: setup, cases and lab-like geometry}
\claim{C-EB-WHY}Three lessons stand out in E-B: an equalizer trained before a switch inside a
packet sees an error as large as the signal after it; a long linear transition spreads the
damage but does not remove it; and with a strong line of sight QPSK tolerates the wrong
configuration, so the timing of a command matters where the RIS path dominates or the margin
is small.

\claim{C-EB-SETUP}In the arc geometry each uncertain distance and angle takes the middle of its
known range, the two receivers stand at different angles at positions that are an engineering
choice (ENG), and $E_s/N_0$ at RX1 is set against the physical noise. A lab-like SIM geometry with one panel of three tiles is a supporting
case. Three assumptions apply to both geometries. The two states of the laboratory cells have
unit amplitude ($|\Gamma|=1$, ASSUMED), because their magnitudes were not measured. During a
transition the response follows the complex-linear path between the states, an engineering
choice (ENG). When the line of sight is blocked, an opaque plate removes the direct paths, and
the RIS hops stay unattenuated because occluders act on the line of sight alone. On the arc
the plate crosses \numEbArcBlockerCrossed{} of the \numEbArcBlockerCells{} segments from the
transmitter to a cell and none from a cell to a receiver, so a physical plate would shadow
most of the incident illumination: the blocked results, PERs and EVMs alike, belong to this
idealized regime, and the cases with the line of sight present are the physically consistent
ones. The packets run at \numEbSps{} samples per symbol and
\numEbRateMsps~MS/s.

\claim{C-EB-TIMING}The arc run fixed its whole E-B2 grid before any point ran
(Table~\ref{tab:eb2grid}): guards of \numEbGuardShortMs, \numEbGuardMs{} and
\numEbGuardLongMs~ms; five jitters; timed commands with three leads, asap and tick-based
commands; the laboratory alphabet with the line of sight present at $E_s/N_0=\numEbEsnoDb$~dB
and at \numEbArcEsnoLowDb~dB (a sensitivity) and blocked at \numEbEsnoDb~dB; and the ideal
1-bit alphabet with the line of sight present at both $E_s/N_0$. On the arc each controller
message carries one command per tile. With the line of sight present at \numEbEsnoDb~dB QPSK
tolerates the wrong configuration, although the EVM of the affected packets rises. With a lead
of \numEbLeadMinMs~ms and the \numEbGuardMs~ms guard, timed commands fail already at
$J=\numEbJitterOneMs$~ms (\numEbArcTimedHalfPerOneLosPct~\% with the line of sight present at
\numEbArcEsnoLowDb~dB and \numEbArcTimedHalfPerOneBlkPct~\% with it blocked); with
\numEbLeadMaxMs~ms they lose no packet at any jitter.

\claim{C-EB-CLOSED}The closed form of Section~\ref{sec:cases-eb} follows from the timeline. With
$\Lambda=\Lambda_0+JU$, $P(\Lambda>x)=\min\{1,\max[0,(\Lambda_0+J-x)/J]\}$, or
$\mathbf{1}[\Lambda_0>x]$ for $J=0$. A packet starts $T_{\mathrm{g}}$ after an asap command is
issued and $T_{\mathrm{g}}/2$ after the requested time of a timed one, which a late timed command
misses by $\Lambda-\ell$; the switch thus lands after the preamble, of
$T_{\mathrm{pre}}=\numEbTpreMs$~ms, with probability $P(\Lambda>T_{\mathrm{g}}+T_{\mathrm{pre}})$
or $P(\Lambda>\ell+T_{\mathrm{g}}/2+T_{\mathrm{pre}})$. A tick-based command waits a further
$W$ for the next tick of period $T_{\mathrm{t}}=\numEbTickMs$~ms. Over a run the guard starts
visit every multiple of \numEbTickPhaseMinUs~$\mu$s (\numEbTickPhaseMaxUs~$\mu$s at the longest
guard) of the tick phase equally often, so $W$ is uniform on $(0,T_{\mathrm{t}}]$ and the
probability is $P(\Lambda+W>T_{\mathrm{g}}+T_{\mathrm{pre}})$, the integral over $U$ of a
piecewise-linear function, which is $1-(T_{\mathrm{g}}+T_{\mathrm{pre}}-\Lambda_0)/T_{\mathrm{t}}$
for $J=0$. With the line of sight blocked, every asap and timed cell with a zero closed form lost
no packet; of the \numEbCfCellsPos{} with a positive one, \numEbCfCellsInCi{} contain it in
their interval and none lies below it beyond its interval. The measured PER exceeds it by up to
\numEbCfMaxExcessPts{} points, most at the \numEbGuardShortMs~ms guard, where many switches fall
in the last part of the preamble and corrupt the channel estimate; switches up to
\numEbPreSafeUs~$\mu$s into the preamble cost nothing. Tick-based cells exceed their closed form
by at most \numEbCfTickExcessPts{} points, except the \numEbTickOverwriteCells{} cells with
$J\ge\numEbJitterOneMs$~ms in which a later command overwrote a pending one before its tick;
the next packet then needs no switch, and the PER can fall below the closed form. With the line
of sight present, a fraction of the exposed packets fail:
\numEbLosLoExposedMinPct--\numEbLosLoExposedMaxPct~\% at \numEbArcEsnoLowDb~dB for both
alphabets, and no cell loses more
than \numEbLosHiPerMaxPct~\% at \numEbEsnoDb~dB. The timing rules are thus consequences of the
semantics, and what the IQ level measures is this conditional loss: where a switch inside the
preamble starts to cost, how the equalizer fails after it, and how much a line of sight
protects.

\claim{C-EB-SERVED}On the served path of
Section~\ref{sec:wc-loop} an asap command became visible after a median of
\numBfourVisPfiftyMinMs--\numBfourVisPfiftyMaxMs~ms, with p99
\numBfourVisPninetynineMinMs--\numBfourVisPninetynineMaxMs~ms and rare outliers of up to
\numBfourVisMaxMs~ms; a timed command there is on time when its lead exceeds the command
latency plus the error of the client's estimate of virtual time.
\claim{C-EB-TICK}The clock of the tick-based semantics starts at $t=0$ and keeps one pending
command per panel. With a timer period of the order of a packet, alignment with the frame
decides its losses, as the closed form shows; a timer period of seconds, as in the cited
5G NR experiments, would expose at most one packet per period.

\claim{C-EB-LABLIKE}In the lab-like geometry (Fig.~\ref{fig:F9}(c), (d)), with the line of
sight blocked, the switch changes the channel of RX1 by
\numEbBlockedChangeDb~dB and \numEbBlockedRotDeg$^\circ$, and \numEbOneErrors{} payload bits
fail for the same switch time. Timed commands with the \numEbLeadMidMs~ms lead lose no packet
up to $J=\numEbJitterOneMs$~ms and \numEbTimedTwoPerThreePct~\% at $J=\numEbJitterMaxMs$~ms.
Asap commands lose \numEbAsapPerOnePct~\% at $J=\numEbJitterOneMs$~ms. Tick-based semantics
lose \numEbTickPerZeroPct~\% without jitter and up to \numEbTickPerMaxPct~\% with it. With the
line of sight present, no semantics loses a packet at \numEbEsnoDb~dB; this geometry was not
run at \numEbArcEsnoLowDb~dB.

\subsection{E-A: protocol record and time scale}
\claim{C-EA-SETUP}The access point sits \numEaApOffsetM~m off the user line, which keeps
the line-of-sight power finite everywhere on the line, and the partition loss gives the RIS
room to help. The partition acts on the line of sight alone, although it crosses the path from
the access point to every cell in every partitioned geometry. A partition that also attenuated
that hop would scale both paths alike and leave the values of the corridor without a
partition at \numEaFcOneGHz~GHz: an optimum gain of \numEaGainAzero~dB and
$\tau_{1/2}=\numEaTauHalfAzero$~s (\numEaTauLvNoPart\,$\lambda/v$). The
hypotheses and their ranges were in the emulator's design document before the sweep: GitHub's
push log (Section~\ref{sec:vv-lab}) records it in the initial push of the emulator repository.
The plan that adds the decision rules and the evaluation rule (SHA-256 \txtEaPlanSha\ldots{}
with LF line ends) was written before the sweep by the authors' record, but committed to the
paper repository after it (commit \txtEaPlanCommit), so no third party dates those parts. One
amendment, made before the full sweep, moved the evaluation of each time step from its start
to its midpoint after a \numEaSmokeS~s IQ run (exact optimum,
$\tau_{\mathrm{SUI}}=\numEaSmokeTauS$~s) had shown the start-of-step rule \numEaSmokeLeftDb~dB
high and the midpoint \numEaSmokeMidDb~dB low; the plan discloses it and a short pipeline test
seen before it. VA-1 therefore compares the IQ runs with a rule chosen after an IQ result had
been seen: it checks the consistency of the evaluator, and it is not a blind test.

\claim{C-EA-EVAL}The engine's \texttt{run\_batch} evaluates one step per interpreter iteration,
too slow for this sweep, so the points come from a vectorized evaluator in the experiment code
that evaluates the same narrowband model. It tabulates the channel of the scene model along the
user line on a grid of \numEaFineGridMm~mm (\numEaFineGridHiMm~mm at \numEaFcTwoGHz~GHz) and
interpolates it complex-linearly, with an error of at most \numEaInterpMaxDb~dB against the
scene model with all cells on, at random times in every geometry; it tabulates the decisions of the
position-driven controllers on their \numEaCoarseGridCm~cm grid, equal to the live controllers'
decisions at \numEaDecisionBarriers{} sampled barriers; it runs URAP and the bandit barrier by
barrier with the engine's random keys; and it evaluates each \numEaStepMs~ms step, split at
switch times, at its midpoint. With the start-of-step rule instead, it matches \texttt{run\_batch}
within \numVaOneVecLeftDb~dB in the VA-1 configurations. VA-1 does not test the heuristic, the
bandit, the \numEaFcTwoGHz~GHz carrier, the other geometries or the time series of the power;
at the received power of its runs, noise moves the mean power by less than
\numVaOneNoiseDiffDb~dB, so its noise-on rows repeat the noise-off test. Every point follows
one random-waypoint trajectory (one seed), shared by all controllers, at one speed, and the RIS
is a single row of $\lambda/4$ cells in a planar geometry; other trajectories, speeds or
two-dimensional panels may change the constants.

\claim{C-EA-WCL}The heuristic of~\cite{Mishra2025WCL}, as run, takes the OFF states as base,
$b=h_{\mathrm{d}}+\sum_nZ_{\mathrm{off},n}$, and $d_n=Z_{\mathrm{on},n}-Z_{\mathrm{off},n}$ as the
change when cell $n$ is switched on. A cell is on when its relative phase
$\delta_n=\arg b-\arg d_n$ lies in an arc of width $\Theta$ at offset $\psi$ from one edge,
$(\delta_n)_{2\pi}<\Theta-\psi$ or $(-\delta_n)_{2\pi}<\psi$, with $(\cdot)_{2\pi}$ reduced to
$[0,2\pi)$. The controller evaluates $|b+\sum_{n\in A}d_n|^2$ on its channel model for
$K=\numEaWclK$ candidates, $\Theta_l=l\pi/(2L)$ for $l=1,\dots,L$ and $\psi=j\Theta/(m+1)$ for
$1\le j\le m\le M$ with $L=\numEaWclL$ and $M=\numEaWclM$, and keeps the best. The letter fixes
$K$ but not the values of $\psi$; this grid is our choice.

\claim{C-EA-RESULT}Table~\ref{tab:eageo} lists every computed geometry: $\tau_{1/2}$ spans
\numEaTauHalfMinS--\numEaTauHalfMaxS~s at \numEaFcOneGHz~GHz and
\numEaTauLambdaVMin--\numEaTauLambdaVMax~$\lambda/v$ over all geometries; at a fixed carrier and
offset it rises with the partition loss, and at a fixed loss it falls with the offset $h$. The
constant thus belongs to the geometry and to the balance between the line of sight and the RIS
path. For isotropic scattering the field and envelope correlations fall to one
half after \numClarkeFieldHalf{} and \numClarkeEnvHalf{} $\lambda/v$~\cite{Clarke1968}. The
rule-of-thumb coherence time $\sqrt{9/(16\pi)}/f_D=\numCoherenceRuleLambdaV\,\lambda/v$ lies
close to the E-A constant, but it is the geometric mean of $1/f_D$ and the envelope
half-correlation time, not a correlation level. The ratio of the two half-lives, \numEaRatio{}
(\numEaRatioLo--\numEaRatioHi), lies \numEaRatioOffsetPct~\% above the wavelength ratio
\numEaRatioPred; the corridor is the same in metres at both carriers, so it is not the same
geometry in wavelengths and exact scaling is not expected. The ranges fixed before the sweep
were \numEaHaOneLoS--\numEaHaOneHiS~s for H-A1 and \numEaHaTwoLo--\numEaHaTwoHi{} for H-A2, so
their support is a weak test. What E-A adds to the classical scaling is the constants for these
geometries, the retention metric and the result for controllers without positions: besides the
bandit (H-A3 supported), URAP gains \numEaGainUrap~dB, and the heuristic
of~\cite{Mishra2025WCL} reaches \numEaGainWclFast~dB at the shortest delay.

\subsection{FM-band dimensioning (E-D)}\label{sec:cases-ed}
\claim{C-ED}This subsection is model-based dimensioning, not validated. Can a focused RIS
restore an FM broadcast link of \numFmLinkKm~km at \numFmFreqMHz~MHz behind an obstruction?
\claim{C-LINEAGE-DATA}The link and the panel of this scenario come from the authors'
unpublished ANTS 2025 submission (Section~\ref{sec:related}); this dimensioning replaces its
result. \claim{C-ED}With a flat-earth two-ray direct path, a coherent knife edge and ground on both RIS hops, the
aperture needed for a RIS path as strong as the direct path is \numFmAreqMin--\numFmAreqMax~m$^2$
over the receiver heights and distances of Fig.~\ref{fig:F12}. At grazing obstruction, over the
same heights and distances, the RIS path of a \numFmPanelMtwo~m$^2$ panel is

with or without ground on its hops, the panel suffices in \numFmPrimarySufficient{} of
\numFmPrimaryEntries{}. A four-ray direct path over flat ground, a model-form sensitivity,
gives \numFmFourRaySufficient{} entries. The picture changes with the direct-path model: with the
experimental ITU-R P.526 smooth-earth path of \sremu{}, which is weaker, the same panel suffices in
\numFmPfiveSufficient{} of \numFmPfiveEntries{} entries. That module is not validated, so the
dimensioning question is decided by the direct-path model rather than by the RIS.

\claim{C-EE-TIMING}The change point of the switching run is the maximum-likelihood change
point of the received IQ, and in every run every engine and transport counter stayed zero.

\claim{C-EE-SPEED}In \txtEeLaptopSweeps{} sweeps of single runs on a laptop (\txtEeLaptopCpu{}, WSL2,
host load not controlled), the lockstep loop with GNU Radio advanced at an RTF of
\numEeLaptopRtfLoMin--\numEeLaptopRtfLoMax{} at \numEeRateMsps~MS/s and
\numEeLaptopRtfHiMin--\numEeLaptopRtfHiMax{} at \numEeLaptopRateHiMsps~MS/s; on an AWS

\claim{C-EE-NOTRUN}srsRAN and OpenAirInterface were analysed but not run. srsRAN~4G pads its
transmit stream to the end of the receive block it is about to read and no further, while
lockstep needs the transmit stream one fractional-delay look-ahead beyond the received
samples; a lockstep srsRAN pair therefore needs a per-peer transmit delay and partial receive
replies, and srsRAN~4G has no package for the Ubuntu release used here. The OpenAirInterface
RF simulator would need a new transport for its own TCP framing~\cite{OAIrfsim}. Both are
future work.
