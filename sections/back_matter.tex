\section*{Code and Data Availability}
\claim{C-OPEN}The emulator \sremu{} and the pipeline that builds this paper from recorded
results (sweep specifications, result files with their provenance, figure scripts) will be
released under an open-source licence (\txtLicence) at \txtRepoUrl{} once all authors and
IIT Bhilai have approved the release; until then they are available from the corresponding
author on request. The exact versions used here, the analysis plans of
Sections~\ref{sec:vv-lab} and~\ref{sec:cases-ea} and their lock records will be archived
with the release (\txtArchiveDoi). The authors' own data files will be released as well
(\txtDataDoi); the digitized laboratory map of Section~\ref{sec:vv-lab} will be released if
the laboratory permits it. Third-party values, such as the curves and points
of~\cite{Tang2022mmWave}, were digitized from published figures and are kept in the emulator
repository with their source and digitization record.
