\section*{Code and Data Availability}
\claim{C-OPEN}The emulator \sremu{} is available at \txtRepoUrl{} under the \txtLicence{}
licence. The pipeline that builds this paper from recorded results (sweep specifications,
result files with their provenance, figure scripts and checks) and the paper sources are at
\url{https://github.com/Galabavamsi/sremu-paper} under the same licence; \texttt{sremu.lock}
names the emulator commit the results come from. The analysis plans of
Sections~\ref{sec:vv-lab} and~\ref{sec:cases-ea}, their lock records and the digitized
laboratory map of Section~\ref{sec:vv-lab} are in the same repositories. Third-party values,
such as the curves and points of~\cite{Tang2022mmWave}, were digitized from published figures
and are kept in the emulator repository with their source and digitization record.
