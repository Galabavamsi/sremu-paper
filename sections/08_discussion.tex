\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Alignment with standards}\label{sec:disc-standards}
\claim{C-STANDARDS}\sremu{} keeps the split of ETSI GR RIS 002~\cite{ETSI_GR_RIS002_v121}
between the RIS controller, which generates control information, and the micro-controller,
which configures the elements (here the engine, at the sample of each receiver). The
general-case path-loss model of GR RIS 003~\cite{ETSI_GR_RIS003_v121} equals K1 for one point
per cell, and the reconfiguration time of GS RIS 008~\cite{ETSI_GS_RIS008}, from the receipt
of a command to the effective configuration, is in \sremu{} the logged command latency plus the
transition. Appendix~\ref{app:survey} maps the ETSI terms to \sremu{} as alignment, not
conformance; the E2 service model for RIS of O-RAN
frameworks~\cite{ElGhalbzouri2025GoSimRIS,Kayraklik2026ORIX} is not implemented.

\subsection{Hooks for learning-based control}\label{sec:disc-ml}
\claim{C-CONTROLLER-API}Learning-based control is outside our scope, but \sremu{} provides
three hooks: one controller object runs in the engine or in an external process with identical
samples (RT-08), with built-in baselines (Appendix~\ref{app:interfaces});
\claim{C-RISENV}an environment, \texttt{RisEnv}, wraps a scenario in the reset/step interface
of Gymnasium~\cite{Towers2024Gymnasium}, with rewards computed on the batch path in this
release; \claim{C-REGRET}and at every step it reports the exact finite-alphabet optimum
(Section~\ref{sec:model-nonideal}) from oracle channel state and the gap to the achieved power,
a regret reference that bounds every built-in controller at every tested position (VA-3).

\subsection{Limitations}\label{sec:disc-limits}
\claim{C-LIMITS}Appendix~\ref{app:model} lists what the release does not model or provide.
Three limits matter most here. The paced mode is soft real time: it counts
deadline misses and does not prevent them. Agreement with MATLAB compares two codes and does
not validate absolute RIS power; validation needs measurements (Section~\ref{sec:vv}). And
mutual coupling enters solely through measured or simulated tables of whole configurations, so
a controller cannot exploit coupling that its tables do not contain.
