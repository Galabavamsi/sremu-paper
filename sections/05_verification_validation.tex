\section{Verification and Validation}\label{sec:vv}

\emph{Verification} asks whether the code computes what the model states; closed forms,
exact properties and an independent code answer it. \emph{Validation} asks whether the model
describes the physical world, which needs measurements; agreement with MATLAB is therefore
verification. Data read off a published figure are labelled DIG with their digitization
uncertainty, and every caption names the classes of its data (Appendix~\ref{app:provenance}).

\subsection{Published RIS path-loss model and measurements}\label{sec:vv-tang}
\claim{C-REFINE}Fig.~\ref{fig:F3}(a) checks a property that published curves cannot test:
when a fixed aperture is cut into more cells, the received power must not change. Far from
the panel, K1 with the default cell model stays within \numRefineFarMaxDevDb~dB of the closed
form for every cell count from \numRefineNmin{} to \numRefineNmax{} (PH-04); near the panel,
the patch model stays within \numRefineNearMaxDevDb~dB of the continuous physical-optics
integral, while one point per cell converges slowly (Appendix~\ref{app:vv}).

\claim{C-ABS-TANG}Tang et al.\ measured the path loss of millimeter-wave surfaces in an
anechoic chamber and fitted a model to it~\cite{Tang2022mmWave}. We extracted the curves and
points of their Fig.~10~\cite{Tang2022mmWave} (DIG; Appendix~\ref{app:tang}) and ran the same \numTangMeasN{} links
through the emulator with the default cell model and the reported unit-cell state and link
budget (REPORTED). The emulator reproduces the
published model curves within \numTangCurveMaxDevDb~dB; since K1 equals that model for one
point per cell, this checks the implementation and bounds the digitization error. Against the
chamber measurements the emulator's received power is low by \numTangMeasBiasDb~dB on
average (SD \numTangMeasSdDb~dB, RMSE \numTangMeasRmseDb~dB; per geometry, with the caveats of
the digitized data, in Appendix~\ref{app:tang}), every measured point lies above it
(Fig.~\ref{fig:F3}(b)), and the
published model is low by \numTangPubMeasBiasDb~dB: the emulator inherits the published
residual. This is agreement of the model class with chamber data for one \numTangFcGHz~GHz
surface in one uniformly coded state in specular geometries; it tests the absolute level and
its dependence on distance and angle, not reconfiguration, steering or near-field structure,
and no switching, transient or timing behaviour of the emulator has been compared with
measurements.

\subsection{Laboratory consistency check}\label{sec:vv-lab}
\claim{C-LAB-DATA}The data set is a laboratory measurement collected by the authors
(Appendix~\ref{app:vv}): the received power at \numLabFreqGHz~GHz on a polar grid of
\numLabCells{} cells in front of a binary RIS of six tiles, whose cell states have phases of
\numLabStateOnePhaseDeg$^\circ$ and \numLabStateTwoPhaseDeg$^\circ$ (magnitudes unknown), surviving
as a colour map that we digitized (DIG, \numLabSigDigMinDb--\numLabSigDigMaxDb~dB per cell). Its geometry is known within ranges, the measurement positions to about
$\pm\numLabPriorPosCm$~cm.

\claim{C-LAB-PRESPEC}We therefore wrote the analysis plan before fitting any RIS model to the
data: \numLabDraws{} geometries drawn from a prior over the unknowns, five models with fitted
offsets, among them line of sight alone (M0), a log-distance fit (M0n) and the laboratory
configuration (M2), fitted on three rings and scored on two, and three decision rules: C1
(level consistent), C2 (the RIS model explains spatial structure better than M0) and C3
(better than a uniform mirror). The plan's hash, which the data loader checks, was pushed after
a descriptive inspection of the map but before any RIS model was fitted to it
(Appendix~\ref{app:vv}): the plan
is pre-specified and hash-locked before unblinding, not blind.
\claim{C-LAB-POWER}In a power analysis on \numLabDecoys{} synthetic maps drawn from M2 itself,
the full analysis found C2 in \numLabDecoyDetections{} of them (\numCiPct\,\% upper limit
\numLabDecoyCiHighPct~\%), far
below the pre-set threshold of \numLabUnderpoweredRate{}, so the study was labelled
underpowered before the data were opened.

\claim{C-LAB-RESULT}The outcome is a null result: the median test RMSE is
\numLabRmseMzero~dB for M0, \numLabRmseMzeroN~dB for M0n and \numLabRmseMtwo~dB for M2, the
RMSE gain of M2 over M0 is \numLabDeltaRmse~dB with a Spearman correlation of
\numLabSpearman, and C1, C2 and C3 all fail, also in every pre-set sensitivity run. Since the
comparison is underpowered, this is not evidence for or against the model.
\claim{C-LAB-LESSON}A diagnostic outside the protocol found that no single extra fact lifts the
C2 detection rate above zero, while the exact as-built tile geometry with positions known to
$\pm\numLabDiagPosMm$~mm gives \numLabDiagRate. At \numLabFreqGHz~GHz the wavelength is
\numLabLambdaMm~mm and the map's structure comes from path differences of a fraction of it, so
a RIS map at this frequency needs its geometry recorded to about a millimetre before it can
separate the models, and even then the detection rate stays near the pre-set threshold: a
measurement-design lesson. The word \emph{validation} is reserved
for a data set with a recorded geometry.

\subsection{CFO estimation and link metrics}\label{sec:vv-cfo}
\claim{C-CFO}With noise off, a receiver oscillator offset swept over \numCfoSweepPoints{}
values within $\pm\numCfoSweepMaxHz$~Hz is recovered by a least-squares phase estimator
within \numCfoLsMaxErrHz~Hz, and the received samples are identical for three client epoch
lengths and through two processes over the native ZMQ data plane (V-CFO-1, V-CFO-3).
\claim{C-CFO-LESSON}Exact rational sample counts avoid a generic pitfall: a time base that
delivers a whole number of samples per tick scales every frequency estimate by the ratio of
the two counts, an error of \numCfoTickScalePct~\% for ticks of \numCfoTickMs~ms at
\numCfoRateSps~S/s. \claim{C-CRB}With the emulator's thermal noise, a periodogram estimator
reaches the Cram\'er--Rao bound of a tone in white Gaussian noise~\cite{Kay1989Freq}, within
the pre-set limit of \numCfoGateFactor{} times the bound at every gated offset (V-CFO-4,
ST-05). \claim{C-BER}Through a static lab-like link, the theoretical bit error rate (BER) of
BPSK and QPSK with genie channel knowledge lies inside the \numCiPct\,\% Clopper--Pearson
interval at all \numBerPoints{} points (study E-F, Appendix~\ref{app:vv}).
