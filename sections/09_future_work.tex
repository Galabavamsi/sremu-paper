\section{Future Work}\label{sec:future}

Several extensions would widen the reach of \sremu{} without changing its time model.
\emph{Unmodified radio software.} A virtual-USRP layer of the kind ACHEM
provides~\cite{Gurses2026ACHEM} would let unmodified UHD applications use the emulator;
running srsRAN or GNU Radio against the raw request--reply mode would turn its stub-peer tests
into an end-to-end check. \emph{Network control.} An adapter for the E2 service model for
RIS~\cite{ElGhalbzouri2025GoSimRIS,Kayraklik2026ORIX} would connect O-RAN applications to the
control plane, and authenticated sockets would allow control across hosts. \emph{Physics.}
Mutual coupling could enter through a mutual-impedance model~\cite{Gradoni2021MutualCoupling}
instead of tables; RIS--RIS paths would allow several interacting surfaces; frequency-dependent
reflection filters would accept signals wider than a table's validity bandwidth; and
polarization and non-reciprocal tables would cover more hardware. \emph{Runtime.} Rate
conversion between streams of different rates, a pipeline resident on a graphics processing
unit (GPU) for large arrays, and vectorized learning environments with IQ backends would
extend the scale of experiments. \emph{Validation.} A second measured dataset with a
documented geometry, including a measured reconfiguration transient, would move parts of the
model card from consistency checks to validation.
