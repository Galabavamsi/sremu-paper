\section{Future Work}\label{sec:future}

\emph{Validation.} \claim{C-NEXT-VALIDATION}The next validation step is the open sub-6\,GHz
data set of Rossanese et al.~\cite{Rossanese2024OpenMeas}: beam scans of a surface with a
documented chamber geometry. Its comparison needs a new analysis plan, locked before the
emulator is run on the data: the measured columns sit on a floor that does not depend on the
configuration, the cells have seven phase states and an absorbing
state~\cite{Rossanese2022WiNTECH} rather than an ideal alphabet, and the descriptive structure
of the data has already been seen, so the comparison can be pre-specified but not blind. A
measured reconfiguration transient would move parts of the model card from consistency
checks to validation. \emph{Unmodified radio software.} A virtual-USRP layer of the kind ACHEM
provides~\cite{Gurses2026ACHEM} would let unmodified UHD applications use the emulator, and
srsRAN would need a lockstep peer that offsets the transmit and receive streams its ZMQ
driver~\cite{srsRANZMQ} ties together (from reading the driver, not tested;
Appendix~\ref{app:cases}). \emph{Network control.} An E2 service model adapter for
RIS~\cite{ElGhalbzouri2025GoSimRIS,Kayraklik2026ORIX} would connect O-RAN applications, and
authenticated sockets would allow control across hosts. \emph{Physics.} Candidates are mutual
coupling through a mutual-impedance model~\cite{Gradoni2021MutualCoupling}, RIS--RIS paths, frequency-dependent
reflection filters for signals wider than a table's validity bandwidth, polarization and
non-reciprocal tables. \emph{Runtime.} Candidates are rate conversion between streams, a pipeline resident on
a graphics processing unit (GPU) for large arrays, a transport outside the engine's
interpreter for the served data plane, and vectorized learning environments with IQ backends.
