\section{Related Work and Gap}\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Coefficient-level simulators and ray tracers}
SimRIS generates statistical millimeter-wave RIS channel matrices by Monte Carlo
simulation with ideal phase control~\cite{Basar2020SimRIS,Basar2021TCOM}. QRIS extends
QuaDRiGa~\cite{Jaeckel2014QuaDRiGa} with unit-cell patterns taken from measurements or
full-wave simulation and with several interacting surfaces; its code is distributed on
request~\cite{Burtakov2023QRIS,QRISPage}. NYUSIM-RIS extends the statistical NYUSIM channel
model~\cite{Habib2023NYUSIMRIS}, and system-level simulators evaluate RIS deployments with
abstracted links~\cite{Hao2023ViennaRIS,Sihlbom2023SLS}. Among ray tracers, Sionna added
RIS in version \txtRepSionnaRisAddedVersion~\cite{SionnaV018Release}, and Sionna
RT~\cite{Hoydis2023SionnaRT,AitAoudia2025SionnaRTReport} removed it in version
\txtRepSionnaRisRemovedVersion; no release up to \txtRepSionnaLatestVersion{} (29 Sep 2026)
has a RIS module~\cite{SionnaRTReleases}. Wireless InSite
describes engineered electromagnetic surfaces with a uniform-ray physical-optics model for
a static configuration~\cite{RemcomEES,DeJong2022EES}. Other ray tracers model RIS
reradiation with ray-based or macroscopic
models~\cite{Vitucci2023RayRIS,Sandh2024RTRIS,Herold2023RISRayTracing,Habib2025CloudRT}, and
a ray-traced model of a deployed surface has been used to choose its phases and checked
against measurements~\cite{Karakelle2026DTRIS}. MATLAB provides rectangular RIS
objects with a user-defined unit-cell element and a reflector that processes sample
vectors offline~\cite{MathWorksRectangularRIS,MathWorksRISCDL}. Electromagnetic models with
mutual coupling~\cite{Faqiri2023PhysFad,Gradoni2021MutualCoupling} and macroscopic
reradiation models~\cite{DegliEsposti2022Reradiation} describe the surface itself rather
than a running link. In this group the RIS configuration is an input to an offline
computation, not a command received while a radio link is running. A systematic review of
RIS simulation models and deployments, co-authored by three of the present authors, covers
the group in more breadth~\cite{Kaur2026SLR}.

\subsection{Network simulators and controller-in-the-loop frameworks}
CoopeRIS couples a far-field RIS gain model to vehicular network simulation and assumes an
oracle controller that knows the vehicle position~\cite{Segata2024CoopeRIS}. ns-3 modules
model 5G and unmanned-aerial-vehicle links assisted by intelligent reflecting surfaces at packet level~\cite{Pagin2022IRSAF,Grieco2024IoDSimIRS},
and ns-3 can draw its channels from Sionna RT, whose current releases have no
RIS~\cite{Zubow2024Ns3Sionna}.
GoSimRIS closes an O-RAN E2 loop between an xApp and a RIS channel simulator through an
E2SM-RIS service model and supports at most \numRepGoSimRisMaxElements{}
elements~\cite{ElGhalbzouri2025GoSimRIS}.
ORIX runs a near-RT loop of \numRepOrixLoopMinMs~ms to \numRepOrixLoopMaxS~s with quantized
phases and a phase-dependent
amplitude model, and neglects mutual coupling and reconfiguration delay~\cite{Kayraklik2026ORIX}.
These frameworks exchange channel coefficients, signal-to-noise ratios or rates, not
waveform samples. A hardware demonstration closes the same loop through the RAN intelligent
controller with a physical surface~\cite{Sahin2025RISmeetsORAN}.

\subsection{IQ-level emulators}
Colosseum combines \numRepColosseumSdrs{} software-defined radios with a channel emulator
built on field-programmable gate arrays (FPGAs) that runs precomputed
scenarios~\cite{Bonati2021Colosseum,Polese2024ColosseumDT}.
Tsampazi and Melodia installed RIS-cascaded channels generated with QuaDRiGa in Colosseum
and ran srsRAN end to end; the RIS phases were optimized before the experiment and are not
reconfigured during it~\cite{Tsampazi2025RISColosseum,Tsampazi2025ORISing}. The OAI
rfsimulator replaces the RF driver and forwards IQ samples over TCP; its documentation
states that it is not designed to keep pace with wall-clock time, and a telnet interface
changes channel models and per-beam gains during a run~\cite{OAIrfsim}. The srsRAN ZMQ
virtual radios exchange IQ samples, and channels can be inserted with a GNU Radio
flowgraph~\cite{srsRANZMQ}. Software-in-the-loop emulators couple the OAI rfsimulator with
ray-traced channel taps~\cite{Iye2025OWDT} or run GNU Radio channel models for swarms of
unmanned aerial vehicles~\cite{Mastronarde2022RFSITL}, and NVIDIA's ray-traced RAN simulation
platform (AODT) has no RIS files in its repository~\cite{AODTRepo}. ACHEM runs unmodified
UHD applications through a virtual USRP,
emulates channels at the IQ level at wall-clock rate with mobility from vehicle
simulators, and reports end-to-end validation against the AERPAW testbed; it has no RIS
model~\cite{Gurses2026ACHEM}. Integrating a RIS operator with such a transport is
discussed as future work in Section~\ref{sec:future}.

\subsection{Open RIS hardware and measurement data}
Open RIS designs publish their hardware, firmware and
measurements~\cite{OpenRISRepo,Heinrichs2023OpenSourceRIS,Chen2026OpenRIS}. Rossanese et al.\
released sub-6\,GHz beam-scan measurements in an anechoic chamber~\cite{Rossanese2024OpenMeas}
of a surface whose cells switch between seven reflective phase states and an absorbing
state~\cite{Rossanese2022WiNTECH}. Such data are the next validation step for \sremu{}
(Section~\ref{sec:future}); this paper compares against the chamber measurements of Tang et
al.~\cite{Tang2022mmWave} (Section~\ref{sec:vv-tang}).

\subsection{TRACE}
\claim{C-TRACE}TRACE~\cite{Kumar2026TRACE}, by some of the present authors, is a
socket-based modular framework that decouples transmitter, radio environment, controller
and receiver through separate control-plane and data-plane interfaces. It evaluates minimum mean-square-error
(MMSE) and orthogonal-matching-pursuit (OMP) channel estimation and a differential channel-aware update (DCAR) on symbol-level
i.i.d.\ Rayleigh channels with Gaussian random-walk and Gauss--Markov evolution, with an
ideal continuous-phase, unit-amplitude surface, and reports a control-plane round-trip
time of \numRepTraceRttMs~ms over UDP~\cite{Kumar2026TRACE}. TRACE and \sremu{} are complementary: TRACE
studies estimation and tracking algorithms on symbol-level channels; \sremu{} provides a
sample-level radio environment with timed RIS control. A TRACE-style controller could
drive \sremu{}'s control plane.

Table~\ref{tab:tools} summarizes the comparison. \claim{C-GAP}We surveyed RIS simulators,
ray tracers, network simulators, controller frameworks, IQ-level emulators and open RIS
hardware and data releases published or released up to 10 Oct 2026, found through Crossref

documentation they link; a tool entered Table~\ref{tab:tools} when it models a RIS or
exchanges IQ samples with radio software, and each feature was read from a primary source
(paper, documentation or code). A tool counts as open when its code is publicly
downloadable. Each of properties
(i)--(iv) of Section~\ref{sec:intro} appears in some row, and no surveyed open tool has all
four. ACHEM has (i) at a stronger level than \sremu{}, since it runs unmodified UHD
applications, and has (iv), but no RIS~\cite{Gurses2026ACHEM}. GoSimRIS and ORIX have
(ii) at the level of channel coefficients~\cite{ElGhalbzouri2025GoSimRIS,Kayraklik2026ORIX}.
QRIS accepts measured or simulated unit-cell patterns, which is close to (iii), but runs
offline~\cite{Burtakov2023QRIS}. In \sremu{}, (iii) is an import path that is tested; this
paper exercises it with reported and assumed states, not with a measured per-state table
(Section~\ref{sec:vv}). The gap is
therefore a combination, and it is dated: a RIS operator could be added to an existing
IQ-level emulator.
