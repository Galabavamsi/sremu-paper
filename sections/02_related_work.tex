\subsection{Coefficient-level simulators and ray tracers}
SimRIS generates statistical millimeter-wave RIS channels with ideal phase
control~\cite{Basar2020SimRIS,Basar2021TCOM}; QRIS extends QuaDRiGa~\cite{Jaeckel2014QuaDRiGa}
with measured or full-wave unit-cell patterns and several surfaces, with code on
request~\cite{Burtakov2023QRIS,QRISPage}; NYUSIM-RIS extends a statistical channel
model~\cite{Habib2023NYUSIMRIS}, and system-level simulators~\cite{Hao2023ViennaRIS,Sihlbom2023SLS}
abstract the links. Among ray tracers, Sionna added
RIS in version \txtRepSionnaRisAddedVersion~\cite{SionnaV018Release}, and Sionna
RT~\cite{Hoydis2023SionnaRT,AitAoudia2025SionnaRTReport} removed it in version
\txtRepSionnaRisRemovedVersion; no release up to \txtRepSionnaLatestVersion{} (29 Sep 2026)
has a RIS module~\cite{SionnaRTReleases}. Wireless InSite models engineered surfaces in a
static configuration~\cite{RemcomEES,DeJong2022EES}, other ray tracers model RIS reradiation
with ray-based or macroscopic
models~\cite{Vitucci2023RayRIS,Sandh2024RTRIS,Herold2023RISRayTracing,Habib2025CloudRT,Karakelle2026DTRIS},
and MATLAB provides RIS objects with a user-defined unit cell that process sample vectors
offline~\cite{MathWorksRectangularRIS,MathWorksRISCDL}. Electromagnetic models with mutual
coupling~\cite{Faqiri2023PhysFad,Gradoni2021MutualCoupling} and macroscopic reradiation
models~\cite{DegliEsposti2022Reradiation} describe the surface rather than a running link. Here the
RIS configuration is an input to an offline computation, not a command received while a link
runs; a systematic review co-authored by three of the present authors covers this group in
more breadth~\cite{Kaur2026SLR}.

\subsection{Network simulators and controller-in-the-loop frameworks}
CoopeRIS couples a far-field RIS gain model to vehicular network simulation with an oracle
controller that knows the vehicle position~\cite{Segata2024CoopeRIS}; ns-3 modules model
links assisted by intelligent reflecting surfaces at packet
level~\cite{Pagin2022IRSAF,Grieco2024IoDSimIRS} or draw channels from Sionna
RT~\cite{Zubow2024Ns3Sionna}. GoSimRIS closes an O-RAN E2 loop between an xApp and a RIS
channel simulator through an E2SM-RIS service model and supports at most
\numRepGoSimRisMaxElements{} elements~\cite{ElGhalbzouri2025GoSimRIS}.
ORIX runs a near-RT loop of \numRepOrixLoopMinMs~ms to \numRepOrixLoopMaxS~s with quantized
phases and a phase-dependent
amplitude model, and neglects mutual coupling and reconfiguration delay~\cite{Kayraklik2026ORIX}.
These frameworks exchange coefficients, signal-to-noise ratios or rates, not waveform
samples; a hardware demonstration closes the loop with a physical
surface~\cite{Sahin2025RISmeetsORAN}.

\subsection{IQ-level emulators}
Colosseum combines \numRepColosseumSdrs{} software-defined radios with a channel emulator
on field-programmable gate arrays that runs precomputed
scenarios~\cite{Bonati2021Colosseum,Polese2024ColosseumDT}. Tsampazi and Melodia installed
RIS-cascaded QuaDRiGa channels in it and ran srsRAN end to end, with RIS phases optimized
before the experiment and not reconfigured during
it~\cite{Tsampazi2025RISColosseum,Tsampazi2025ORISing}. The OAI rfsimulator forwards IQ
samples over TCP, is by its documentation not designed to keep pace with wall-clock time, and
changes channel models and per-beam gains during a run through a telnet
interface~\cite{OAIrfsim}; the srsRAN ZMQ virtual radios exchange IQ samples, and a GNU Radio
flowgraph can insert channels~\cite{srsRANZMQ};
software-in-the-loop emulators add channel models to such transports (Appendix~\ref{app:survey}).
ACHEM runs unmodified UHD applications through a virtual USRP,
emulates channels at the IQ level at wall-clock rate with mobility from vehicle simulators,
and reports end-to-end validation against the AERPAW testbed; it has no RIS
model~\cite{Gurses2026ACHEM}.

\subsection{Open RIS hardware and measurement data}
Open RIS designs publish hardware, firmware and
measurements~\cite{OpenRISRepo,Heinrichs2023OpenSourceRIS,Chen2026OpenRIS}. Rossanese et al.\
released sub-6\,GHz chamber beam scans~\cite{Rossanese2024OpenMeas} of a surface with seven
reflective phase states and an absorbing state~\cite{Rossanese2022WiNTECH}, the next
validation step for \sremu{} (Section~\ref{sec:future}); this paper compares against the
chamber measurements of Tang et al.~\cite{Tang2022mmWave} (Section~\ref{sec:vv-tang}).

\subsection{TRACE}
\claim{C-TRACE}TRACE~\cite{Kumar2026TRACE}, by some of the present authors, is a
socket-based modular framework that decouples transmitter, radio environment, controller
and receiver through separate control-plane and data-plane interfaces. It evaluates minimum mean-square-error
(MMSE) and orthogonal-matching-pursuit (OMP) channel estimation and a differential channel-aware update (DCAR) on symbol-level
i.i.d.\ Rayleigh channels with Gaussian random-walk and Gauss--Markov evolution, with an
ideal continuous-phase, unit-amplitude surface, and reports a control-plane round-trip
time of \numRepTraceRttMs~ms over UDP~\cite{Kumar2026TRACE}. TRACE and \sremu{} are complementary: TRACE
studies estimation and tracking algorithms on symbol-level channels; \sremu{} provides a
sample-level radio environment with timed RIS control. A TRACE-style controller could
drive \sremu{}'s control plane.

\subsection{Gap}\label{sec:related-gap}
\claim{C-GAP}We surveyed RIS simulators, ray tracers, network simulators, controller
frameworks, IQ-level emulators and open RIS hardware and data releases published or released
up to 10 Oct 2026, found through Crossref and arXiv searches, the reference lists of the papers
found and the repositories and documentation they link; a tool entered the comparison when it
models a RIS or exchanges IQ samples with radio software, each feature was read from a primary
source (paper, documentation or code), and a tool counts as open when its code is publicly
downloadable. Table~\ref{tab:toolsmain} shows that each of properties (i)--(iv) of
Section~\ref{sec:intro} appears in some row and no surveyed tool has all four (\sremu{} is
not yet open). ACHEM
has (i) at a stronger level than \sremu{}, since it runs unmodified UHD applications, and
(iv), but no RIS~\cite{Gurses2026ACHEM}; GoSimRIS and ORIX have (ii) at the level of channel
coefficients~\cite{ElGhalbzouri2025GoSimRIS,Kayraklik2026ORIX}; QRIS accepts measured unit-cell
patterns, close to (iii), but runs offline~\cite{Burtakov2023QRIS}. In \sremu{}, (iii) is a
tested import path that this paper exercises with reported and assumed states, not with a
measured per-state table (Section~\ref{sec:vv}). The gap is therefore a combination, and it is
dated: a RIS operator could be added to an existing IQ-level emulator
(Section~\ref{sec:future}).

\claim{C-LINEAGE}An earlier in-house simulator by the authors was used
in~\cite{Mishra2025WCL,Mishra2025NCC}; \sremu{} is an independent new implementation.

sweep of Section~\ref{sec:vv-cfo} and the FM scenario appeared in the authors' unpublished
