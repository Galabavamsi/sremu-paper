Some details of the tools of Section~\ref{sec:related} complete the table. Wireless InSite
describes engineered electromagnetic surfaces with a uniform-ray physical-optics model for a
static configuration~\cite{RemcomEES,DeJong2022EES}, and a ray-traced model of a deployed
surface has been used to choose its phases and checked against
measurements~\cite{Karakelle2026DTRIS}. The ns-3 modules model 5G and unmanned-aerial-vehicle
links~\cite{Pagin2022IRSAF,Grieco2024IoDSimIRS}, and ns-3 can draw its channels from Sionna RT,
whose current releases have no RIS~\cite{Zubow2024Ns3Sionna}. The hardware demonstration
of~\cite{Sahin2025RISmeetsORAN} closes the loop through the RAN intelligent controller. The
Colosseum channel emulator is built on field-programmable gate arrays
(FPGAs)~\cite{Bonati2021Colosseum}. Software-in-the-loop emulators couple the OAI rfsimulator
with ray-traced channel taps~\cite{Iye2025OWDT} or run GNU Radio channel models for swarms of
unmanned aerial vehicles~\cite{Mastronarde2022RFSITL}, and NVIDIA's ray-traced RAN simulation
platform (AODT) has no RIS files in its repository~\cite{AODTRepo}.

\claim{C-STANDARDS}Table~\ref{tab:standards} maps the terms and models of the ETSI documents
on RIS to elements of \sremu{}. In the split of GR RIS 002~\cite{ETSI_GR_RIS002_v121},
controllers decide, and the engine applies their commands with the panel's control
granularity, quantizer and failure map at the sample of each receiver. The reradiation integral of GR RIS
003~\cite{ETSI_GR_RIS003_v121} has the factor $j$ that K1 keeps; its mutually coupled antenna
model and its polarized elements are outside the model of \sremu{}. The parameters of GS RIS
008~\cite{ETSI_GS_RIS008} appear as fields of the response tables and of the panel
capabilities (Appendix~\ref{app:interfaces}), and the default duration of the transition is
the panel's reconfiguration time. The modulated-signal quality of GS RIS 008, the error
vector magnitude (EVM), is measured by an RX application on received samples;
Section~\ref{sec:cases} uses it.
