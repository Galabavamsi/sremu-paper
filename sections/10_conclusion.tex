\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

\claim{C-TIMED-CMD}\sremu{} makes the moment at which a RIS configuration takes effect part of
the experiment: a command lands at a requested and logged sample on every receiver,
\claim{C-LOCKSTEP-EXACT}and lockstep runs with controllers in the loop are deterministic. For
the design of RIS controllers, the case studies point to three rules.
\claim{C-EB-TIMING}Where a switch inside a packet costs bits, a command must carry its switch
time with a lead above its worst latency, or its whole latency must fit inside the guard
between packets: in the guard slice studied, untimed commands lost packets once the worst
latency exceeded the guard. \claim{C-EA-RESULT}Position-driven
control needs position reports younger than a fraction of $\lambda/v$: in the corridor
studied, half of the RIS benefit was gone after \numEaTauOverLambdaV\,$\lambda/v$.
\claim{C-EC}Designing for the states of the surface, rather than for an ideal alphabet,
recovered up to \numEcDesignPenaltyMaxDb~dB for the laboratory states (magnitudes assumed) in
the geometries studied.

For testing, the results teach three lessons. \claim{C-WC-PACEDCMD}Command timing must be
measured on the path that the controller will use: over the served control socket the visible
latency reached a p99 of \numBfourVisPninetynineMaxMs~ms, above the latencies modelled in
lockstep, so a lead that suffices in a lockstep study need not suffice there.
\claim{C-ABS-LAB}A blinded comparison with a laboratory map, analysed under a plan
hash-locked before unblinding, \txtVLabOutcome; \claim{C-LAB-LESSON}a RIS map at
\numLabFreqGHz~GHz needs its geometry recorded to about a millimetre before it can test the
spatial structure of a model. \claim{C-ABS-TANG}The absolute level, in contrast, can be tested
against third-party chamber data, where the emulator and the published model that it
reproduces show nearly the same offset from the measurements.
\claim{C-NEXT-VALIDATION}The next step is a comparison with open sub-6\,GHz measurements of a
reconfigurable surface under a newly locked analysis plan.
