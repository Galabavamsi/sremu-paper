\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \sremu{}, a software-only emulator of RIS-assisted radio environments at the
level of complex-baseband samples. \claim{C-TIMED-CMD}RIS configurations are timestamped
commands from external controllers, applied at an exact and logged sample on every receiver,
and element responses are tables imported with their provenance. \claim{C-LOCKSTEP-EXACT}Exact
rational time, closed-form per-sample evaluation and keyed random numbers make lockstep runs
deterministic, also with controllers in the loop, and make their received samples identical
bit for bit under any client chunking, any cut by events or barriers, and across process
boundaries. \claim{C-CONTRIB-2}The area-tied kernel equals three published forms of the RIS
path-loss model and reproduces published model curves within \numTangCurveMaxDevDb~dB, and
the emulator as a whole passes \numOracleCount{} analytic oracle checks. \claim{C-ABS-LAB}A
pre-registered comparison with a laboratory measurement collected by the authors
\txtVLabOutcome; \claim{C-LAB-LESSON}its lesson is that a RIS map at \numLabFreqGHz~GHz needs a
geometry recorded to about a millimetre. \claim{C-CRB}Frequency-offset estimates
through the emulator are exact with noise off and reach the Cram\'er--Rao bound with its
physical noise. \claim{C-ABS-PACED}In wall-clock-paced mode on \txtPacedPlatform{} the
emulator sustains \numPacedLinks{} links at \numPacedMSps~MS/s with a deadline-miss rate below
\numSustainMissRate. \claim{C-ABS-EB}In one case study, in a simulated geometry modelled on
our laboratory with RIS switches in \numEbGuardMs~ms guards between packets, \txtEBResult. The
difference appears where a switch at the wrong time costs bits: at
$E_s/N_0=\numEbArcEsnoLowDb$~dB or with the line of sight blocked. With the line of sight
present at \numEbEsnoDb~dB, no semantics lost more than \numEbArcLosHighPerMaxPct~\% of the
packets. \claim{C-ABS-EA}In the other, position-driven binary control under signaling delay
\txtEAResult. The intra-frame result depends on when a command takes effect
within the sample stream, which coefficient-level and block-level tools do not represent. The
delay result runs on the coefficient path; a subset run at the sample level agrees with it within
\numVaOneMaxDiffDb~dB.
