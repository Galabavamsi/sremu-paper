\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

\claim{C-TIMED-CMD}In \sremu{} a RIS command takes effect at a requested and logged sample on
every receiver, \claim{C-LOCKSTEP-EXACT}and lockstep runs with barrier controllers, in
process or over the control socket, are deterministic. \claim{C-EB-TIMING}With the line of sight blocked, the packet error rate followed
the closed form of the semantics: commands applied on arrival lost no packet while their worst
latency stayed below the guard plus the \numEbTpreMs~ms preamble, timed ones below their lead
plus half the guard plus the preamble; with a line of sight, the waveform set the cost.
\claim{C-EA-RESULT}Position reports must be younger than a fraction of $\lambda/v$, as Doppler
decorrelation predicts~\cite{Clarke1968,Jakes1974}: half of the RIS benefit was gone after
\numEaTauLambdaVMin--\numEaTauLambdaVMax\,$\lambda/v$ across geometries
(\numEaTauOverLambdaV\,$\lambda/v$ in the primary one). \claim{C-EC}Designing for the laboratory
states rather than an ideal alphabet recovered up to \numEcDesignPenaltyMaxDb~dB in the geometries
studied (magnitudes assumed).

\numBfourRateLowHz--\numBfourRateMidHz~commands/s, at least \numBfourExactOkMinPct~\% of
commands timed \numBfourLeadOkMs~ms ahead took effect at their exact sample, though page
deadlines failed our Sustainable rule; leads must cover the measured latency of the
controller's path. \claim{C-ABS-LAB}A laboratory comparison
under a pre-specified plan hash-locked before unblinding \txtVLabOutcome;

offset the emulator inherits; no switching or timing behaviour was compared with measurements.
