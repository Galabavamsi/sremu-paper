\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \sremu{}, a software-only emulator of RIS-assisted radio environments at the
level of complex-baseband samples. \claim{C-TIMED-CMD}RIS configurations are timestamped
commands from external controllers, applied at an exact and logged sample on every receiver,
and element responses are tables imported with their provenance. \claim{C-LOCKSTEP-EXACT}Exact
rational time, closed-form per-sample evaluation and keyed random numbers make lockstep runs
deterministic, also with controllers in the loop, and make their received samples identical
bit for bit under any client chunking, any cut by events or barriers, and across process
boundaries. \claim{C-CONTRIB-2}The area-tied kernel equals three published forms of the RIS
path-loss model and reproduces published model curves within \numTangCurveMaxDevDb~dB, and
the emulator as a whole passes \numOracleCount{} analytic oracle checks. \claim{C-ABS-LAB}A pre-registered comparison with
digitized laboratory data \txtVLabOutcome. \claim{C-LINEAGE}Counting samples exactly moves the
slope of the estimated carrier-frequency offset of the legacy engine from \numCfoSlopeLegacy{}
to \numCfoSlopeFixed{}. \claim{C-ABS-PACED}In wall-clock-paced mode on \txtPacedPlatform{} the
emulator sustains \numPacedLinks{} links at \numPacedMSps~MS/s with a deadline-miss rate below
\numSustainMissRate. \claim{C-ABS-EB}In one case study, a RIS switch inside a packet
\txtEBResult. \claim{C-ABS-EA}In the other, position-driven binary control under signaling
delay \txtEAResult. Both results depend on when a command takes effect within the sample
stream, which coefficient-level and block-level tools do not represent.
